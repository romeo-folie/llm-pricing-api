# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers and technical decision-makers comparing language models, benchmark evidence, pricing, and operational tradeoffs. They need to understand which claims are supported, how current the supporting data is, and when the available evidence is insufficient.

## Product Purpose

LLM Pricing API provides an auditable way to compare model pricing and capabilities. The interface should help users make evidence-backed choices without presenting missing, stale, or weak evidence as certainty.

## Brand Personality

Precise, transparent, and quietly technical. The product should feel calm, credible, and direct.

## Anti-references

Avoid glossy generic SaaS presentation, crypto aesthetics, neon-heavy interfaces, decorative gradients, and exaggerated claims. Do not obscure uncertainty behind promotional language or ornamental visuals.

## Design Principles

1. Make evidence and uncertainty visible.
2. Prefer clear comparisons over decorative presentation.
3. Use familiar product-interface patterns that keep attention on the data.
4. Separate measured facts from interpretation and recommendation.
5. Keep dense technical information readable and navigable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard navigation and reduced-motion preferences. Never communicate status or meaning through color alone.
